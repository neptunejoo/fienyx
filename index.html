Core Service

Translation

Every translation we produce is guided by two uncompromising ideals: faithfulness and felicity. Faithfulness means the source meaning is rendered accurately, nothing added, nothing lost. Felicity means the result reads naturally in the target language, as if it were written there to begin with.

Every translation is also culturally adapted, localized for the right demographics, context, and subject matter, so your message connects rather than just converts.

Intelligent Transcription

This is not simple dictation. It is Intelligent Data Capture. We deliver time-stamped, speaker-identified transcripts built for your specific use case: SEO optimization, database ingestion, or accessibility compliance.

Where AI-generated transcripts hallucinate, our human-audited process guarantees verified accuracy. For legal depositions, medical records, or academic research, that difference is everything.

Editing Service

Light Editing

A focused pass through your text that catches surface-level errors and smooths rough edges, while fully preserving your original voice and structure. Ideal for writing that is nearly there and needs a final push toward clarity and correctness. Also suitable for AI-generated drafts that require a quick accuracy and grammar pass before use.

Request a Quote
  • Grammar, spelling, and punctuation
  • Minor syntax errors
  • Confusing or inconsistent word and phrase usage (with comments)
  • Errors in common facts and general knowledge
  • Words inappropriate for the subject or intended audience
  • Guidance on quotation permissions and citations
  • Surface-level grammar and tone pass for AI-generated content
Substantive Editing

Heavy Editing

A comprehensive structural and linguistic overhaul addressing cohesion, coherence, parallelism, and overall clarity from the ground up. We strengthen logical flow, eliminate redundancy, and tailor your content for maximum cultural relevance to your target audience.

We distinguish between Technical Editing (precision and clarity) and Creative Editing (tone and style), and apply whichever, or both, your project demands. This service is also particularly effective for AI-generated content that needs significant restructuring, factual verification, and a natural human voice.

Request a Quote
  • Grammar, spelling, capitalization, format, and punctuation
  • Language that hinders effective communication
  • Illogical flow, repetition, and contradictory content
  • Titles, illustrations, tables, numbers, and references
  • Cross-cultural optimization for target demographics
  • Rhetoric and figurative language choices
  • Pronouns and connectors for smooth, continuous reading
  • Deep restructuring and humanization of AI-generated drafts
  • Hallucination and factual error detection in AI content

A multi-pass Linguistic Quality Assurance audit that goes far beyond a spell-check. We verify grammar, formatting consistency, and brand-voice alignment to certify that your document is truly publication-ready.

We also offer final-pass proofreading specifically for AI-generated content, catching the subtle artifacts, unnatural phrasing, and inconsistencies that automated tools tend to leave behind.

06 · Common Questions

Everything you need to know
before you start.

Answers to the questions clients ask most often, from pricing and turnaround to confidentiality and file formats.

Translation converts text from one language to another as accurately as possible. Localization goes further: it adapts the content for a specific cultural context, adjusting idioms, date/number formats, images, tone, and cultural references so that the material feels native to the target audience, not merely translated.

At Fienyx, every translation project includes a localization pass. We never deliver text that merely converts words; we ensure the meaning, intent, and cultural resonance land exactly as you intended.

Confidentiality is non-negotiable for us. Every project is handled under strict internal data-handling protocols, and team members are bound by professional confidentiality obligations. Files are never shared outside the project team.

For legal, medical, or corporate clients who require it, we are happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before work begins. Simply request one when submitting your project application.

We offer Express Turnaround for clients with tight deadlines. Standard delivery is 3–5 business days per 1,000 words; express projects can be completed in 1–2 business days, subject to team availability and project complexity.

Express projects may carry an additional rush fee. Please mention your deadline in the project application form and we will confirm feasibility and pricing within 1–2 hours on business days.

We accept most common document formats including .DOCX, .PDF, .XLSX, .PPTX, .TXT, .RTF, .ODT, and .IDML (InDesign). For transcription, we accept audio and video files such as .MP4, .WAV, .MP3, .M4A, and streaming links.

Deliverables are returned in the same format as your source file unless you request otherwise. Transcripts can be delivered as plain text, .SRT/.VTT caption files, JSON, or structured XML.

Our standard unit is the finished page for translation, editing, and proofreading (one A4 page at Arial 12pt, double-spaced, approximately 225 to 250 words, as defined by HPI). Transcription is priced per audio or video minute. Per-word equivalents are also shown on our pricing cards for easy reference.

Final pricing depends on the language pair, subject matter complexity, turnaround time, and service type. We provide a clear, itemized quote before any work begins. No hidden charges, no surprises.

Whether your content was written by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other AI tool, we review it for accuracy, coherence, natural flow, and brand-voice alignment before it goes live.

We also offer Post-Editing of Machine Translation (PEMT) for AI or machine-translated drafts. A linguist reviews and corrects the output to publication standard, often at a lower cost than full human translation while still delivering verified, natural-sounding results.

One important note: we do not generate AI content on your behalf. Our role is to be the human expert who ensures that whatever AI produces actually meets the quality bar your audience expects.
